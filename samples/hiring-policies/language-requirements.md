# Language requirements - Northstar (fictional)

## Working language

English is the working language of Northstar. Every role requires professional English (B2 or higher).

## Additional languages

- Roles that support DACH customers (Customer Success, Sales, Support) require German at C1 or higher.
- Roles that support Iberian customers require Spanish or Portuguese at C1 or higher.
- For product, design and engineering roles, additional languages are never required. They count as a plus when the posting lists them as preferred, for example when the product is localised for the target market.

## Assessing language

- Do not ask candidates where they were born or what their first language is. Ask them to confirm their working proficiency in the languages the job requires.
- Resumes written in German or Spanish are valid applications. Evaluate them on their content and record the language in the scorecard.
- When a role requires a language, run the matching part of the interview in that language.
