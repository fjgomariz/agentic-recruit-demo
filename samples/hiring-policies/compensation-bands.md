# Compensation bands - Northstar (fictional)

Effective 1 January 2026. All figures are gross annual base salary. Bands are reviewed every January.
Every offer must sit inside the band for the level of the role. Offers above the band maximum need
written approval from the People Director and cannot be promised before approval.

## Design and research

| Level | Title examples | UK (GBP) | Germany / Spain / Portugal (EUR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L3 | Product Designer, UX Researcher | 48,000 - 62,000 | 42,000 - 56,000 |
| L4 | Senior Product Designer, Senior UX Researcher | 64,000 - 82,000 | 56,000 - 74,000 |
| L5 | Staff Designer, Design Systems Lead | 84,000 - 104,000 | 74,000 - 94,000 |
| L6 | Head of Design | 105,000 - 130,000 | 95,000 - 118,000 |

The Senior Product Designer role in Product & Design is level L4.

## Notes

- Salary expectations stated by a candidate are inputs, not commitments. Compare them with the band before the first interview and tell the candidate early when the expectation is outside the band.
- Candidates relocating between UK and EU bands are paid under the band of their country of employment.
