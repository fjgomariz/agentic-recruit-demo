# Interview process - Northstar (fictional)

Every candidate goes through the same stages for a given role family. Recruiters must not skip stages
and must record the structured scorecard within 24 hours of each interview.

## Design and research roles

1. **Recruiter screen** (30 minutes): motivation, availability, salary expectation against the band, languages.
2. **Hiring manager interview** (45 minutes): scope, impact and collaboration.
3. **Portfolio review** (60 minutes): the candidate presents two projects; panel of two designers.
4. **Research and craft exercise** (take-home capped at 3 hours, paid): reviewed by the panel with the scorecard.
5. **Cross-functional interview** (45 minutes): a product manager and an engineer.
6. **Values interview and references.**

## Engineering roles

1. Recruiter screen, 2. technical screen, 3. system design, 4. pairing session, 5. values interview.

## Rules

- Use the structured scorecard for every interview. Free-text only feedback is not accepted.
- A candidate who withdraws or is rejected can be considered again for another role after 6 months. Keep the data only with the candidate's consent and delete it within 24 months.
- Notice periods longer than 3 months must be flagged to the hiring manager at the screen stage.
