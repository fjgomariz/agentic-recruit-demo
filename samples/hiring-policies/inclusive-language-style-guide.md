# Inclusive language and job posting style guide - Northstar (fictional)

Use this guide when writing or reviewing job postings, outreach messages and candidate feedback.

## Voice

- Write in plain English, second person ("you will"), present tense. Keep sentences under 25 words.
- Describe outcomes, not personalities. Say "you will own the research roadmap", not "we want a rockstar".

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| rockstar, ninja, guru | expert, specialist |
| young and dynamic, digital native | motivated, curious |
| manpower | workforce, staffing |
| culture fit | values alignment |
| native English speaker | fluent English |
| aggressive, dominant | ambitious, decisive |

## Requirements

- List no more than 6 required qualifications. Everything else is "preferred".
- State the salary band in every posting for roles in the UK, Germany and Spain.
- Do not require a degree unless the law does. Say "or equivalent experience".
- Never mention age, gender, nationality, marital status or photographs in a posting or in feedback.
- Every posting must include the sentence: "We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds and will adapt the process for accessibility needs."

## Candidate feedback

- Evaluate only job-relevant skills and evidence. Do not penalise career breaks.
- Cite concrete evidence from the resume or the interview for every score.
