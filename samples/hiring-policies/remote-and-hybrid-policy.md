# Remote and hybrid work policy - Northstar (fictional)

Northstar is hybrid by default. The rules below apply to every employee unless a job posting says otherwise.

## Locations and expectations

- **London office:** teams in Product & Design are expected on site on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
- **Hybrid within the UK:** employees must live within 90 minutes of the London office.
- **Remote within the EU:** allowed for roles posted as "Remote EU". Employees must be based in Germany, Spain, Portugal, France or the Netherlands, where Northstar has an employer of record. Other EU countries need approval.
- **Relocation:** the relocation package is up to GBP 6,000 for UK moves and EUR 8,000 for international moves. It is offered only to L4 and above.

## Working hours

- Core overlap hours are 10:00 - 16:00 UK time for every team.
- Employees outside the UK keep their local public holidays.

## Right to work

- Northstar sponsors visas only for roles at L4 and above and only for the UK. Candidates must state their right to work status in the application. Recruiters must not ask about nationality or ethnicity.
