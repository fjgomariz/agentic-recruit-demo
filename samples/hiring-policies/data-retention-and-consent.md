# Data retention and candidate consent - Northstar (fictional)

## Talent pool

- Candidates who were not selected may be added to the talent pool only if they gave explicit consent in the application form.
- Pool data is kept for at most 24 months from the last contact. After that it is deleted automatically.
- A candidate can ask at any time to see, correct or delete their data. Requests are answered within 30 days.

## Using the pool for new roles

- Recruiters may search the pool for a new role. The search is limited to people who consented and whose data is still within retention.
- The first contact must explain why the candidate was found, link to the privacy notice and offer an opt-out.
- Do not use the pool to infer protected characteristics. Matching must rely on skills, experience and stated availability.

## AI-assisted screening

- AI output is advice. A human recruiter makes every decision and records the reason.
- Every AI score must cite evidence from the resume. Scores without evidence are discarded.
